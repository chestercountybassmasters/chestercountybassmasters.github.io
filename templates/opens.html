Open to everyone

The Upper Bay Open team series

A top regional team series, hosted in Maryland out of Anchor Marina on the North East River and the Susquehanna Flats. Anglers come from Maryland, Delaware, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and even Virginia and New York. In 2026, 106 teams fished the series.

Rules

Chester County Bass Masters may refuse entry to anyone disqualified from a club or other tournament for unsportsmanlike conduct. Entry fees are forfeited if conduct during a tournament leads to disqualification.

  1. Five fish limit per boat, largemouth or smallmouth.
  2. Minimum length 15 inches from March 1 to June 15.
  3. Minimum length 12 inches from June 16 on.
  4. A short fish brought to the scales costs a 1 pound penalty and the fish.
  5. Each dead fish takes 8 ounces off the total weight.
  6. The dead fish penalty does not reduce the weight of a lunker.
  7. Artificial lures only. No live bait.
  8. All current Maryland fishing and boating laws apply.
  9. A factory-installed, aerated livewell of more than 10 gallons.
  10. Livewells must be able to aerate for the whole tournament.
  11. All boats must carry all safety equipment.
  12. All boats are inspected before launching.
  13. No alcohol or illegal drugs.
  14. Late return: 1 pound a minute, up to 15 minutes.
  15. Disqualified at 15 minutes late.
  16. The late penalty also applies to the lunker.
  17. Unsportsmanlike conduct may bring disqualification.
  18. Keep a courtesy distance from other boats.
  19. No trolling as a way of fishing.
  20. First tiebreaker: the most fish alive.
  21. Second tiebreaker: the largest fish.
  22. The Tournament Director's decision is final.
  23. No fishing after your boat number is turned in.
  24. Liability insurance on your boat is your responsibility.
  25. Solo entries are allowed.
  26. The ramp fee is the boater's responsibility.
  27. Life vests must be worn when the gas motor is running.
  28. The kill switch must be attached when the boat is in gear.
  29. Weigh bags are provided.
  30. Off limits: inside the line from the fishing pier at the town park to the flag pole just above the first cut as you leave the marina (the blue house), including all of the Anchor Marina and Nauti Goose docks. [Map of the off-limits line]

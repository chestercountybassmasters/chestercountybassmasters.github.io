CCBM Tournament #1
North East River
April 9, 2022

The 2022 season starts out with a slow bite. Big bags are out there but they were tough to find.
We had 27 anglers looking to start the year off right but only 7 found fish to bring to the table.

Lou Croce ended last season just shy of the AOY title but starts 2022 on top.
Lou only brought 2 fish to the scales but they were the right ones. His total weight of 8.41 lbs was enough to give him the win but he also took home Lunker honors as well as the Rolling Lunker award with a 5.68 lb Largemouth!!

Paul Sirochman rolls a 2021 AOY right into a 2nd place finish and is looking to repeat a spectacular 2021 campaign.
His 2 fish for 5.16lb may not represent a big bag but net him big 2nd place points, as he hopes to repeat.

New old member Russell Davis shows home and takes home a check for his trouble. He showed up at the scales with a solid spring Largemouth that tipped the scales at 4.91 lbs.

CCBM Tournament #2
Nanticoke River
April 23, 2022

Young guns show up strong at the 2nd event of the year.
20 of the 23 anglers brought fish to the scales, with more than half of them bringing 4 or more fish.

Adam Harkness takes home his first win as a member of CCBM. He had a great day catching them on a Buzzbait all day long and culled to a bag that was 10.20 lb and just misses out on the day's Lunker award.

Travis Nale brings in a strong bag and takes home a 2nd place finish. His limit of bass weighed in at 9.79 lb.
He pairs this with a win last year to start off his time with CCBM!!

Tom Yaw gets a jump to an early lead in the Rider Of The Year race after taking home a 3rd place finish. His bag weighed in at 8.68 lb on a day when many caught them!

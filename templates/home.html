The Upper Bay Open team series

A top regional team series, hosted in Maryland on the North East River and the Susquehanna Flats. Anglers come from Maryland, Delaware, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and even Virginia and New York. In 2026, 106 teams fished the series.

It is a route to the Bassmaster Team Championship, and from there one angler goes on to the Bassmaster Classic, the biggest tournament in bass fishing.

  • Entry$100 a boat
  • Lunker pool$10 of the entry, all paid back
  • Place prizes80% of the other $90
  • Side pot, optional$60
  • Teams1 or 2 anglers, 5 fish
  • DaySafe light to 2:30 pm

Sign up online ahead of time or at the ramp, from an hour before launch. Sponsors put up prizes well down the standings, and nine boat and equipment makers pay contingency cash.

The club

We share what we know

We fish hard and we tell each other how we caught them. Members of any skill level are welcome, boaters and riders alike. If tournaments sound like fun and you need a back seat to ride in, we are the club for you.

  • 1976The Delaware County Bass Ambassadors start fishing together.
  • 2004Chester County Bass Masters is founded.
  • 2010The two clubs merge, and have grown into one of the largest bass clubs in the tri-state region.

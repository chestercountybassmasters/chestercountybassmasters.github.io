The club

We fish hard and share what we know

Chester County Bass Masters is for bass anglers of any skill level, with a boat or as a rider. If tournaments sound like fun and you need a back seat to ride in, we are the club for you.

How to join Email the club

Our mission

We are dedicated to sportsmanship, fair play, honest competition, conservation and teaching young people to fish for bass. We follow the rules of our parent organization, B.A.S.S. Nation, and we treat every member with respect.

We share our fishing experience openly with each other, so that every member learns and gets better.

Our history

  • 1976The Delaware County Bass Club starts as the Delaware County Bass Ambassadors, and has been active every year since.
  • 2004Chester County Bass Masters is founded.
  • 2010The two clubs merge and keep the Chester County name. We have grown into one of the largest bass clubs in the tri-state region.

What membership gives you

  • Twelve club tournaments a year, as a boater or a rider
  • District, state and team events, and a path to the Bassmaster Classic
  • Insurance for club events through B.A.S.S. Nation
  • The members' message board, where the tournament signups and results are

Part of B.A.S.S. Nation

B.A.S.S. Nation is a network of local clubs whose members fish tournaments and support conservation and youth programs. Anglers who qualify through club, state and regional tournaments can reach the national championship and, from there, the Bassmaster Classic.
